# Example - Dar al Islam vs Dar al Harb as authority claim

This is a worked example, from a highlight you used on Nov 8, 2026. Delete it or keep it as a model. Do not treat the example as a finished note until you rewrite the claim in your own words.

## Claim

The Dar al Islam vs Dar al Harb binary is better read as a claim about who gets to police authority than as a map of the world.

## Question this note answers

What makes the us/them split do work: territory, or authority?

## Evidence

- Source 1: Benny Morris, Righteous Victims. Islam, like Christianity and Judaism, traditionally divides the world into us and them, Dar al Islam vs Dar al Harb.
- Source 2: See Literature Note for Righteous Victims in `07 Library` for the verbatim highlight.

## Why it matters

If the boundary is about authority, the same binary can move inward, to who counts as a true believer, not just outward to territory.
